SecureClaw — First-Time Setup
==============================

1. Drag SecureClaw.app to your Applications folder.
2. Double-click to open it.

macOS will show a warning because SecureClaw isn't from
the App Store. Here's how to allow it (one time only):

  → Open System Settings
  → Click "Privacy & Security"
  → Scroll down — you'll see "SecureClaw was blocked"
  → Click "Open Anyway"
  → Enter your password when prompted

After that, double-click works normally every time.

Privacy: SecureClaw runs 100% on your Mac. It makes zero network
connections — your files never leave your computer.

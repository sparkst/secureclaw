# Awareness Training: Recognizing Role-Confusion Attacks

Goal: train staff to spot role-confusion attempts in shared documents.

Attackers often try to convince an AI assistant to assume a different
persona that lacks the original guardrails. Common surface signals:

- Instructions that reframe the assistant as having a different role.
- Sudden shifts to all-caps assertions about identity.
- Unicode tricks that obscure the framing words from filters.

This tutorial avoids reproducing the literal trigger phrases on the
recommendation of the legal team. Instead we describe the structural
indicators that defenders should monitor.
